Regulations
RULES & REGULATIONS

🎾 Match Format

  • T20 format – 20 overs per side
  • Max 4 overs per bowler
  • Minimum 5 different bowlers required
  • Powerplay: max 2 fielders outside circle (6 overs)
  • Super Over in case of tie
  • White tennis ball used across all matches

👔 Eligibility

  • Only corporate employees allowed
  • Valid employee ID must be submitted
  • Latest payslip/PF copy for verification
  • No contract/vendor/intern players allowed
  • Entry confirmed only upon full payment
  • Fake documents = immediate disqualification

👥 Team Rules

  • 15-player squad registered at enrollment
  • 11 players + 1 Super Sub per match
  • No new players in semi/final stages
  • Replacements only in league stage with approval
  • Background verification required for new players
  • New player must be verified 3 days before match

⏱️ Timing & Reporting

  • Captain/Vice-Captain report 30 min before
  • 8+ players report 15 min before match
  • Over limit: 1 hr 20 min + 5 min grace
  • Late over = fielding restrictions, 5 inside 30-yard
  • Next batsman must enter within 90 seconds
  • Between overs: 60 seconds only

👕 Attire

  • Dinamalar sponsored jersey mandatory
  • Black/dark track pants preferred
  • White pants NOT allowed for bowlers
  • Proper sports shoes required
  • Spike shoes strictly not allowed
  • Helmet mandatory for batsmen & keeper

⚡ No-Ball & Extras

  • Free-hit after every no-ball
  • Byes & leg-byes allowed
  • Mankad allowed without warning
  • Penalty runs for late overs/misconduct
  • Umpire decision is final
  • Misconduct = ban from future tournaments
